1. Data controller

Muhammad Adeel Mirza Jaral is the data controller for dashcam recordings made in this private hire vehicle. The data controller is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) under registration/reference number 00014688605.

2. Purpose of recording

The dashcam is used in the interests of public safety, crime detection and crime prevention, and for legitimate private hire transport purposes. These purposes include protecting the driver and vehicle; deterring and investigating crime or antisocial behaviour; and providing evidence relating to collisions, complaints, insurance claims, disputes or legal proceedings.

3. What the dashcam records

The dashcam is configured to record externally facing road footage only. The cabin-facing camera is disabled, passengers inside the vehicle are not intentionally recorded, and audio recording is disabled.

External footage may capture pedestrians, drivers and other road users, vehicles, registration marks, road locations, dates, times and other information visible within the external cameras' fields of view.

4. Lawful basis

Where UK data protection law applies, the processing is based on the data controller's legitimate interests in maintaining safety and security, protecting property, preventing or detecting crime, responding to incidents, and establishing, exercising or defending legal or insurance claims.

5. Retention and automatic overwrite

Routine dashcam footage is retained for a maximum of 4 hours on a continuous rolling loop, after which the oldest routine footage is automatically overwritten by newer recordings.

Specific footage may be preserved beyond the normal retention period where it is required in connection with an accident, complaint, crime, insurance claim, police or licensing request, legal proceeding, data protection request, or another lawful and necessary purpose. Any footage preserved for such a purpose will be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for that purpose.

6. Security, access and disclosure

Access to footage is restricted to the data controller and any person or organisation with a lawful and necessary reason to receive it. Footage is not routinely published and will not be uploaded to social media by the data controller.

Relevant recordings may be disclosed where lawful and necessary to the police, City of Wolverhampton Council or another licensing or regulatory authority, insurers, courts, legal representatives, or another person or organisation with a lawful entitlement to receive them.

7. Your data protection rights

If you are identifiable in dashcam footage, you may have rights under UK data protection law. Depending on the circumstances, these may include the right to request access to your personal data, the right to object to processing, and rights relating to erasure or restriction of processing.

These rights are not absolute and may be limited where the law permits, for example where footage needs to be retained for an accident, crime, insurance claim, legal proceeding, or the rights and freedoms of other people.

Because routine footage is automatically overwritten after a maximum of 4 hours, requests relating to dashcam footage should be made as soon as possible and should include the approximate date, time and location of the recording so that relevant footage can be identified if it still exists.

8. Right to object

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. If you object, please contact the data controller using the details below and explain the circumstances of your objection. The request will be considered in accordance with applicable UK data protection law.
