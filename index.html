1. Data controller

Muhammad Adeel Mirza Jaral is the data controller for dashcam recordings made in the private hire vehicle. The data controller is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) under registration/reference number 00014688605.

2. Purpose of recording

The dashcam is used for legitimate private hire transport purposes, including the safety and security of the driver, passengers and vehicle; deterrence and investigation of crime or antisocial behaviour; and the provision of evidence relating to collisions, complaints, insurance claims, disputes or legal proceedings.

3. Information that may be recorded

The dashcam may record images of passengers, pedestrians, drivers and other road users, together with vehicles, registration marks, road locations, dates, times and other information visible within the camera’s field of view.

4. Lawful basis

Where UK data protection law applies, the processing is based on legitimate interests in maintaining safety and security, protecting property, responding to incidents, and establishing, exercising or defending legal or insurance claims.

5. Four-hour retention and automatic overwrite

Routine dashcam footage is retained for a maximum of 4 hours. The system operates on a continuous rolling loop. After approximately 4 hours, the oldest footage is automatically overwritten by new recordings.

Footage is not kept beyond the normal 4-hour period unless a particular recording is required because of an accident, complaint, crime, insurance claim, police request, legal proceeding or another lawful and necessary purpose. Any footage preserved for such a purpose will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary.

6. Access and disclosure

Footage is not routinely published or disclosed. Relevant recordings may be shared where lawful and necessary with the police, licensing or regulatory authorities, insurers, courts, legal representatives, or another person or organisation with a lawful entitlement to receive it.

7. Data subject requests

A person captured in the footage may have rights under UK data protection law, including the right to request access to personal data relating to them. Because routine recordings are automatically overwritten after 4 hours, any request relating to dashcam footage should be made as soon as possible and should identify the approximate date, time and location of the recording.
